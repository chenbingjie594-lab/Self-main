# Project positioning

- **Plastic_Bomo** is the primary development/self-built dataset.
- **DeepPCB** is a frozen public generalization dataset.
- DeepPCB Stage5–13 are retained as boundary/generalization evidence only and must not drive first-module design or selection.
- MSDF-v3, FrozenResidualAdapter, TMVB, gradient allocation, RCC, and CPT are retired/unsupported on DeepPCB; no DeepPCB-specific rescue is authorized.
- BootstrapGuard remains an independent second innovation and is not merged into Stage14A generator diagnosis.

The former cross-dataset Stage14A headroom proposal is cancelled. Stage14A is the Plastic_Bomo Generator Bottleneck Audit.
