# Experiment 10 public artifact policy

This directory contains code, frozen protocols, aggregate gradient statistics, metric tables, audit summaries, and the final report.

Server-only material is intentionally excluded: original/generated images, model weights, Inception feature arrays, training/update logs, absolute paths, complete sample/conditioning identities, per-sample gradient rows, and the detailed generation manifest. The published conflict matrix, aggregate gradient summary, and generation-integrity audit preserve the reviewable conclusions and counts without disclosing sample-linked metadata.
